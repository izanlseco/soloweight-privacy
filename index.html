SoloWeight does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

  • The weights and dates you enter, your deleted-entries history, and your optional height and target weight.
  • This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app does not do

  • The app has no internet permission and never sends data anywhere.
  • No account or sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • We, the developer, have no access to your data.

Export and backups

  • You can export your data as a CSV file. Where that file goes (for example, your files or an app you choose to share it with) is entirely up to you.
  • If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup in your Google account. This is done by your phone's operating system under Google's privacy policy, not by SoloWeight.

Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Google, as the payment processor, passes us limited order information (such as an order ID and your country) so we can confirm your purchase if you contact support; we never see your card or payment details. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payment data.

If you email us for support

  • Purpose: we use the email address and content of your message only to answer your question.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing support to people who contact us, or your consent by emailing us.
  • Retention: we keep support conversations for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, then delete them.
  • Your rights: under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete or limit the use of your support emails; write to soloappscontact@gmail.com.
  • Complaints: if you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the app, empty the deleted-entries list, or uninstall the app or use "Clear storage" in your phone's settings to remove all data.

Children

SoloWeight is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
